Description

The Villa Grao in Castellón is a very spacious and cozy accommodation which has air conditioning and parking. Great location on the FIRST LINE OF THE BEACH.

It has 4 bedrooms, which are equipped with a double bed and seven single beds. It also has two bathrooms equipped with the necessary bathroom items and utensils, a kitchen fully equipped with essential appliances for the guest's stay, a living room with sofa and TV, dining room, private, private pool, balcony and terrace for personal enjoyment.

This holiday villa, located in Grao de Castellón and on the beachfront, offers stunning views beautiful and an ideal environment for relaxation and rest in front of Playa del Palmeral. It is located a 4-minute walk from the bus stop and 700 meters from the main attractions of the area. Its location facilitates access to various restaurant services and supermarkets.

Some places of interest are the dog beach, the coastal park, street workout park, blue trail coastal park, pinar park, pinar pool, golf club, planetarium, port of Castellón, surf school and yacht club.
